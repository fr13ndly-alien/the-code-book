# Where the Vietnamese edition differs from our English pages

The Vietnamese pages are retold from *Mật mã: Từ cổ điển đến lượng tử* (NXB Trẻ, 4th printing 2014, translated by Phạm Văn Thiều and Phạm Thu Hằng). It is a translation of the **full** edition of *The Code Book*, while our site's chapters follow the abridged edition.

The writers found the places below where the Vietnamese edition says something different from our English page. In each case **the Vietnamese page keeps our English page's fact**, unless the note says otherwise. Some are translation slips in the edition, and others are differences between the full and abridged editions. Page numbers are the edition's printed pages.

Collected on 15 November 2026. Worth checking against the English full edition before acting on any of them.
